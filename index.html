What sets us apart

Your Partner

We view ourselves as an extension of your team, a partner in the truest sense. We seek no recognition for our work but are quietly known by leading decision makers globally. Our high-touch approach necessitates working with only a select group of sophisticated clients. Achieving your goals is the only measure of success.

Global & multifaceted

We bring a unique blend of experiences to each engagement, leading with skills derived from strategic advisory, institutional finance, global business development, and entrepreneurial ventures. Working at the intersection of geopolitics and finance, between emerging markets and developed ones we advise decision-makers on complex risks and opportunities facing their businesses.

Integrity

Our reputation is our greatest asset. Clients trust us with their most sensitive issues, fears and aspirations. They do so because we’ve built a legacy of professionalism where honesty, transparency, confidentiality and discretion are core pillars within our client engagements. Clients deserve candid and actionable guidance, a clear understanding of our process, the utmost care with sensitive business priorities and unwavering protection of their identities.

About Robb

Meet Robb

Robb brings 25 years of significant and substantive leadership and entrepreneurial experience in building and leading investment, advisory, and technology firms in the U.S. and abroad. An outstanding communicator, Robb is adept at originating and developing powerful relationships with private equity firms, corporations, sovereigns, high-net worth individuals, family offices and pension funds. He has a proven track record securing new business, raising capital, obtaining sustainable revenue streams, restructuring businesses, driving profitability and managing clients. Robb has extensive expertise advising corporations and investors on hedge funds, emerging market risk, geopolitical issues and anti-corruption matters.
